## Findings (pick numbers to fix; unmentioned numbers stay untouched)

### 01 — Arrow on every CTA, purpose nowhere written [MEDIUM, R-08]
Evidence: `components/Book.tsx:416,426` (`← Prev`/`Next →`), `book-pages/ProjectPage.tsx:52` (`ArrowUpRight` on all 4 project links), `book-pages/ContactPage.tsx:22` (`ArrowRight` on Reach Me), `book-pages/TitlePage.tsx:24` (tagline `→`).
Reason: arrows are the button identity everywhere, not a signal anywhere.
Fix: add 2 lines to `DESIGN.md` (arrows = direction cues: prev/next movement, external-link exit) or drop the arrow on one CTA class.

### 02 — Infinite status-dot pulse, MOTION dial undeclared [MEDIUM, R-19]
Evidence: `app/globals.css` `.project-status-dot` (`dot-pulse 3s ease-in-out infinite`); no `ENERGY/RHYTHM/MOTION` line in `DESIGN.md`.
Reason: perpetual motion with no written UX purpose, and motion can't be judged against a dial that isn't declared.
Fix: declare `Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 2` in `DESIGN.md` frontmatter + one line for the pulse (live-status signal) or make the dot static.

### 03 — Identical cards with no written hierarchy reason [LOW, R-14]
Evidence: `book-pages/ColophonContent.tsx` (3 capability cards + 5 process steps, same size/padding/layout).
Reason: uniformity is defensible for equal-weight items, but the reason is not written down.
Fix: one line in `DESIGN.md` (equal-weight services share one card treatment; flagship differentiation not needed).

### 04 — No explicit dials, no Design Read [LOW, R-31/R-37]
Evidence: `DESIGN.md` frontmatter has colors/typography/spacing but no `Dial:` line.
Reason: keystone rule wants every major decision (including rhythm/motion level) articulable in one line.
Fix: add `Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 2` + one Design Read sentence; nothing else changes.

### 05 — `outline: none` on programmatic focus landings [LOW, R-32]
Evidence: `app/globals.css:1137,1290,1641` (cover h1, title h2, contact h2 with `tabIndex={-1}`, focused after each flip).
Reason: sighted keyboard users get no visible landing mark when focus moves to the new page heading.
Fix: keep `outline: none` for pointer users but show a `:focus-visible` mark, or record it as a deliberate exception (headings aren't tab stops; the live region announces the page).

### 06 — Book + email button need JS, no fallback note [LOW, C-4/R-26]
Evidence: `app/page.tsx` (book state, hash routing, `mailto` attached in `useEffect`); no `<noscript>` anywhere.
Reason: without JS the reader sits on the cover and Reach Me is a dead button, though all copy is in the DOM for crawlers.
Fix: add a small `<noscript>` note ("this monograph needs JavaScript; contact via …"), or record JS-required as a documented constraint.

### 07 — Empty Print section [LOW, C-4]
Evidence: `app/globals.css:1821` is a `Print` header with zero rules; the 3D spreads print as stacked artifacts.
Reason: a shipped state (paper) that was started and never finished.
Fix: delete the header, or add 5-line print rules (flat pages, ink on white, hide controls/shadow).

### 08 — Decorative banner comments repo-wide [LOW, code skill/R-31]
Evidence: `═══`/`───` banners in `app/globals.css`, `components/Book.tsx`, `app/page.tsx`.
Reason: decoration around labels reads as generated; labels themselves carry the information.
Fix: normalize to plain sentence-case section lines (code untouched, comments only), or record banners as the repo's deliberate convention.

### 09 — Widest mono tracking unexplained [LOW, R-06]
Evidence: `app/globals.css` cover hint `0.32em`, title-brand `0.3em` vs standard `0.08–0.12em`.
Reason: wide tracking is used at exactly two print-convention spots (cover hint, spine note) but DESIGN.md never says so.
Fix: one line in `DESIGN.md` (widest tracking reserved for cover/spine furniture).
